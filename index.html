Not another general Pokédex.

This project focuses on the names themselves: roots, puns, sound symbolism, cultural references, what a native speaker may hear, and how each localization changes the idea.

Current scope

Generation I species. English, French, and Japanese first. No sprites. No attempt to duplicate encyclopedic resources that already exist.

    Next major feature

    Kanto living-dex routes

    The route guides will grow from the same data rather than becoming a separate generic walkthrough. They will tell you what to catch, where to catch it, how many copies a living dex requires, what can be missed, and which encounters provide useful EVs.

    First planned guide: FireRed / LeafGreen
    Planned after the Gen I name foundation is stable
    • Route-by-route and floor-by-floor encounter planning
    • French (English; Japanese) naming throughout
    • EV-aware defeat, catch, and flee advice
    • Version exclusives, evolution quantities, trades, fossils, and irreversible choices
    • Red, Blue, and Yellow guides added within the Kanto phase—not every game at once

    The goal is a Pokémon language and completion companion: deep enough for people who love words, practical enough to keep beside an active playthrough.
