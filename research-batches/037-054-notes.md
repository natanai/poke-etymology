# Audit notes: #037–#054

Reviewed 2026-07-29 under `RESEARCH_METHOD.md`.

## Decisions preserved

- Rokon and Kyukon keep the fox-cry readings while stove/grudge echoes remain secondary possibilities.
- Feunard is treated as a compressed fire/fox/nine construction rather than pretending every boundary is equally audible.
- Purin is grounded first in the ordinary Japanese word for pudding; weaker petite/pretty theories are omitted.
- Pukurin and Wigglytuff retain multiple plausible roundness/toughness components.
- Golbat keeps gore, ghoul, and gulp as alternatives because no single first root is securely established.
- Oddish, Kusaihana, Nazonokusa, Parasect, Diglett, Dugtrio, and Psyduck contain comparatively transparent constructions.
- Kongpang, Mimitoss, Morphon, and Meowth remain explicitly plausible where their internal boundaries are not fully transparent.
- Persian is treated primarily as the cat-breed name shared internationally, not as an invented three-language pun.

## Additional language references

Where useful, entries supplement the standard official Pokédex, PokéAPI, and Bulbapedia links with CNRTL, Académie française, Merriam-Webster, Kew Science, and the Cat Fanciers’ Association.
