# Xatu English naming attribution

Jeff Kalles identifies **Xatu** as his English name suggestion while assisting with the Generation II localization. He explains that the team was seeking an X-initial species name and that he derived it from *xat*, which he described as a carved Indigenous American pole.

The runtime credit is therefore a species-specific override in `naming-credits-generation-ii.js`, rather than the ordinary Generation II team default.

This attribution does not establish every proposed visual or cultural association for Xatu. The etymology entry reports Kalles's explanation narrowly and avoids inferring a specific Indigenous nation, object category, or artistic tradition beyond his own wording.
