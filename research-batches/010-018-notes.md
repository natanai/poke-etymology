# Audit notes: #010–#018

Working decisions for Caterpie through Pidgeot. Published entries live in `verified-research-010-018.js`.

- Caterpie and Butterfree retain essentially the same Japanese/English constructions; describe continuity rather than implying independent reinvention.
- Transel: `transform + cell` is primary; resemblance to `randoseru` (school backpack) remains secondary.
- Weedle: `worm + needle` is plausible, but the worm component is much less transparent to English speakers than needle.
- Spear: the literal English loanword is primary; `Apis` reversed is a secondary possibility.
- Pidgey/Pidgeotto/Pidgeot retain competing English possibilities rather than forcing one definitive segmentation.
- The French bird line deliberately escalates `cool → coups → carnage` while retaining `roucouler`.
