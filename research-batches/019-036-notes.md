# Audit notes: #019–#036

Reviewed 2026-07-29.

- Rattata’s `rat + attack` analysis is strengthened by its pre-release naming history; `rat-a-tat` remains secondary.
- Raticate’s second element remains unresolved between `eradicate` and `masticate`.
- Arbo is treated as a probable reversed `boa`; the exact Japanese route remains uncertain.
- Pikachu is the strongest claim in this batch because Atsuko Nishida directly explained `pika` and `chu`. French and English are recorded as unchanged borrowings, not new local puns.
- Sandpan’s `sand + pangolin` analysis is plausible rather than confirmed.
- The Nidoran family is deliberately conservative. `needle`, `rhino`, gendered endings, and `king/queen` are recorded with local confidence; proposed `二度 nido` wordplay is never upgraded beyond possibility.
- Clefairy’s Japanese `Pippi` → `pixie` relationship remains plausible; Clefable’s Japanese `Pixy` is a direct loan.
- French and English entries that retain a Japanese/international name explicitly say so instead of inventing language-specific roots.
