# FireRed / LeafGreen Living Dex research — Route 9 through Route 16

**Reviewed:** 2026-07-31  
**Guide stages:** `route-9` through `route-16-north`

This record explains the completion decisions behind the second guide extension. It is not user-facing walkthrough prose.

## Scope

The chunk begins after Lt. Surge and ends after the northern Route 16 detour. It intentionally stops before the Celadon Game Corner, Rocket Hideout, Celadon Gym, and the return to Pokémon Tower.

The six stages are:

1. Route 9;
2. Route 10 North;
3. Rock Tunnel;
4. Lavender Town and Route 8;
5. initial Celadon City setup;
6. Route 16 North.

## Route 9

Route 9 introduces no new required wild family at a better practical rate than elsewhere. The guide therefore keeps this stage focused on route access and the single-use TM40 Aerial Ace.

Verified items:

- TM40 Aerial Ace is in the southwest corner;
- a hidden Ether, Chesto Berry, and Rare Candy are available;
- Route 9 leads directly to Route 10 North and its Pokémon Center.

Primary walkthrough reference:

## Route 10 North

### Voltorb quantity

Voltorb is 40% in the grass at levels 14, 16, or 17 in both versions. Two copies are required simultaneously:

- one Voltorb;
- one to evolve into Electrode at level 30.

The guide records +1 Speed EV for Generation III Voltorb.

### Everstone

Professor Oak’s aide in the Pokémon Center gives an Everstone after at least 20 different species have been caught. It is useful but not required for living-dex progress, so the task is optional.

## Rock Tunnel

### Machop family

Generation III encounter tables:

- 1F: Machop 15%, levels 16–17;
- B1F: Machop 10%, level 17.

The guide recommends 1F and three copies for Machop, Machoke, and the trade evolution Machamp.

### Onix family

Generation III encounter tables:

- 1F: Onix 5%, levels 13 or 15;
- B1F: Onix 10%, levels 13, 15, or 17.

The guide recommends B1F and two copies:

- one retained as Onix;
- one reserved for the post-National-Pokédex Metal Coat trade into Steelix.

Steelix was added to `reference-data.js` so its localized name remains clickable without adding it to the visible Generation I index.

### Rock Slide tutor

The Youngster in the northwest section of B1F teaches Rock Slide once. This is an irreversible move-tutor decision, so it receives both a stage warning and a task row.

### Items

The normal route can collect Repel, Escape Rope, Pearl, Revive, and Max Ether. None is required for route or living-dex completion, so the combined sweep is optional.

## Lavender Town and Route 8

### Why Pokémon Tower waits

Without the Silph Scope, wild Pokémon in Pokémon Tower are unidentified ghosts and cannot be caught. Entering early can provide battles, but it does not advance the living dex and creates unnecessary repeated climbing. The guide therefore postpones the tower until after the Celadon Rocket Hideout.

References:

- https://www.pokepedia.fr/Fant%C3%B4me_%28Tour_Pok%C3%A9mon%29
- https://www.pokepedia.fr/Tour_Pok%C3%A9mon

### Route 8 version exclusive

Generation III Route 8 grass:

- FireRed: Growlithe 20%, levels 15–18;
- LeafGreen: Vulpix 20%, levels 15–18.

Two copies are required for each two-stage stone family. Route 8 is preferred over Route 7 because the equivalent version-exclusive encounter is 10% on Route 7, making Route 8 the cleaner practical catch.

The fenced grass requires Cut. The same patch contains hidden Leppa, Lum, and Rawst Berries; these are optional.

## Celadon City setup

### Gift Eevee

The Celadon Mansion rooftop gift is one level-25 Eevee per save. The guide asks the player to keep it unevolved for now because evolving the only copy removes the base form until breeding or another trade is available.

For the Kanto-only living set, four simultaneous copies are eventually needed:

- Eevee;
- Vaporeon;
- Jolteon;
- Flareon.

For the guide’s Generation II-aware family planning, six are eventually needed by adding Espeon and Umbreon.

FireRed and LeafGreen do not have a clock, so Eevee cannot evolve into Espeon or Umbreon in these games. A compatible time-enabled Generation III game is required for those evolutions, followed by trading them back. Breeding becomes available at the Four Island Day Care in the postgame.

Espeon and Umbreon were added to `reference-data.js` so their localized names remain clickable without appearing in the visible Generation I index.

### Tea and Coin Case

Tea from the elderly woman on Celadon Mansion 1F opens the four Saffron City gates. The Coin Case from the restaurant is included now because the next guide leg must address Game Corner prize Pokémon.

### Repeatable evolution stones

Fire Stone, Thunder Stone, Water Stone, and Leaf Stone are repeatable purchases on the Department Store’s fourth floor for $2100 each. The guide explicitly names this because players should not treat these four stone types as finite one-save resources.

## Route 16 North

### Doduo quantity

Generation III Route 16 grass contains Doduo at 35%, levels 18, 20, or 22. Two copies cover Doduo and Dodrio; Doduo evolves at level 31.

### HM02 Fly

The woman in the northwest house gives HM02 Fly after the player reaches the hidden northern path with Cut. Lt. Surge’s Thunder Badge already enables Fly outside battle.

### Amulet Coin

Professor Oak’s aide in the gate gives the Amulet Coin after 40 different caught species. It helps fund Game Corner and Department Store purchases but is not required, so it remains optional.

The sleeping Snorlax continues to block Cycling Road until the Poké Flute is obtained after Pokémon Tower.

## UX and performance decisions

- Existing task and stage IDs remain unchanged.
- Six stages are appended, so saved stage indices remain stable.
- Optional hidden-item and threshold-reward tasks do not count toward required progress.
- All Pokémon names use `[[ID]]` tokens and retain localized direct links.
- No runtime request, observer, timer, polling, framework, image, or repeated background process was added.
- The complete chunk remains static JavaScript consumed during the existing one-pass render.
