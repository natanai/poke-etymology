# Poké Etymology

A mobile-first Pokémon language and completion companion.

The project focuses on **why Pokémon are named what they are** across languages: roots, puns, literal meanings, cultural references, native-speaker impressions, localization choices, and confidence levels for uncertain etymologies.

## Current release

- Generation I research seed: National Pokédex #001–#025
- English, French, Japanese, and Japanese romanization
- comparative etymology notes and confidence labels
- EV yields retained for future playthrough guidance
- responsive red/white/black interface inspired by the visual language of early Pokémon games
- no sprites and no attempt to duplicate a general-purpose Pokémon encyclopedia

## Long-term direction

The same data will eventually power Kanto living-dex routes, beginning with FireRed / LeafGreen and later covering Red, Blue, and Yellow. Guides will combine encounter planning, living-dex quantities, missable choices, EV-aware advice, and multilingual name notes.

See [PROJECT_GOALS.md](PROJECT_GOALS.md) for the detailed product boundaries and order of work.
